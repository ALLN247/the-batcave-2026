## The Helper Trap

Most educators operate as **Helpers.** A Helper does the task in front of them — grades this stack, writes this lesson, answers this message — one at a time, reactively, as it comes. Helpers are exhausted by Sunday because Helpers never stop being needed.

An **Operator** runs a system. An Operator has already built the structure — the charter, the templates, the rhythm — so that the work that used to take all weekend gets handled inside the week, and Sunday goes back to being Sunday.

The shift from Helper to Operator isn't about working harder or caring less. It's about building once and running it every week after that.
