## Section 3: Audience Profile

### Primary Persona: "Ms. Renee" — The Overloaded Urban Educator

**Who she is:** A Black or Latina classroom teacher at an under-resourced urban charter or faith-based school. 3–15 years of experience. Has 4–5 preps. Serves high-need students. Genuinely loves teaching but is running on empty.

**Her situation:** She's grading at 11pm, planning at 5am, and still feeling behind. Her district has no AI support. Her colleagues think AI is cheating. She doesn't have $200/month for all the tools she sees on social media.

**Her top pains:**
- No time for meaningful lesson design
- No AI support or PD budget for tools that actually work
- Feels like she's falling behind while wealthier school teachers get every resource
- Afraid AI will make her feel dumb or irrelevant

**What she wants:**
- A system that saves her 5–10 hours per week
- To feel competent and current — not left behind
- To make a real impact on her students without burning out
- Tools that understand HER classroom, not some generic suburban classroom

**What she fears:**
- AI replacing her value
- Investing time in tools that don't stick
- Being judged by admin or parents for using AI

**How she talks:** Direct. A little tired. Hopeful but skeptical. Will say "this actually works" once she sees it. Responds to community, not corporate.
