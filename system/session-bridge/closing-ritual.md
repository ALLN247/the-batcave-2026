# Closing Ritual — Session State Capture Protocol

*Routing: Focus OS / session end → AI.G*

**When to run**: Before you close any project session. Before you switch to another project. Before you step away.
**Time required**: 2 minutes.
**The rule**: You do not close the project until the PSF is updated. The PSF is the handoff — to future you.

---

## The Closing Prompt

Paste this to AI.G at the end of a session, with the PSF and your current work attached:

---

> **AI.G — Session Closing. Update the PSF for this project:**
>
> Based on what we did in this session, update the following fields in the PSF. Give me the updated text for each field — I'll paste them in.
>
> 1. **Last session date**: Today's date.
>
> 2. **What I actually did last session**: Summarize what actually moved in this session — specific, not vague. Not "worked on the project." What changed?
>
> 3. **Current state of the project**: Where is the work stopped right now, exactly? Be specific enough that I could hand this to someone else and they'd know where to pick it up.
>
> 4. **Next Action**: What is the ONE thing I should do when I return to this project? Make it specific enough that future me can start without thinking. If there are multiple candidates, pick the one that unblocks the most.
>
> 5. **Session log entry**: One line. Date + what moved. For the table.
>
> Then ask me one question: *"Before you close — what are you excited about? What would you want future you to remember about why this matters?"* Capture my answer verbatim for the Motivation Capture field.

---

## Without AI (manual version — 2 minutes, no chat needed)

Answer these five questions and write the answers directly into the PSF before closing:

1. What did I actually do today? (1-2 sentences)
2. Where exactly is the project stopped right now?
3. What is the ONE thing to do when I return?
4. What files or links do I need open when I come back?
5. What am I excited about right now — before I close?

Fill in the Session Log row. Close the file. Walk away.

---

## The moment test

Before you close, ask yourself: *"If I came back to this in two weeks with no memory of today — would my PSF get me back on track in under 60 seconds?"*

If the answer is no, the closing isn't done. Spend 90 more seconds on it.

---

## Why the Motivation Capture section exists

Motivation is time-sensitive. It's high right after a good session and near zero when you return to a cold project days later. The Motivation Capture field stores the version of you that was excited about this work — so future you can borrow that energy when the file feels foreign.

Write it in present tense, in your own voice, while you still feel it.
