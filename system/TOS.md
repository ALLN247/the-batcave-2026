## PART 3 — PERSONAL OPERATING SYSTEMS (LOS — Life OS)

**LOS Principle**: Businesses scale to the health of the operator. Fix the person first. Always.

The five pillars are not productivity categories. They are the domains of the person. If any pillar collapses, everything downstream degrades. These are checked before any business decision is made.

---

### FAITH OS

**Purpose**: Maintain the covenant relationship that grounds every decision, every dollar, and every design.

| Domain | Practice |
|---|---|
| Daily | Morning prayer before any screen · One scripture, one application · The Faith & Focus Note as the day's anchor |
| Weekly | Covenant renewal before the Sunday System runs · Sabbath protected — no exceptions |
| Ongoing | Every piece of content carries a Faith & Focus Note · Character decisions are pre-decided — no negotiation in the moment |

**Metrics**: Is prayer happening before work? (Y/N daily) · Did the day's work align to the mission? (Y/N daily) · Am I leading from obedience or from fear? (Weekly reflection)

**Scripture anchors**: Proverbs 4:7 / 1 Corinthians 9:24-27

**Decision filter**: *Does this decision require me to compromise character for capability? If yes, the answer is no.*

---

### FITNESS OS

**Purpose**: Maintain the physical capacity to sustain the mission long-term. Energy is infrastructure. A depleted operator is a degraded system.

| Domain | Practice |
|---|---|
| Daily | Movement — non-negotiable, even if abbreviated · Recovery: sleep, water, nutrition basics · Energy check (1–10) before major work blocks |
| Weekly | Structured training session(s) per ALL'N Fitness programming · Body audit: what needs attention? |

**Metrics**: Sleep quality (1–10 subjective) · Energy average across the week · Movement: days completed vs. planned

**Decision filter**: *Does adding this commitment protect or sacrifice my physical capacity to lead?*

---

### FAMILY OS

**Purpose**: Lead the most important people in the building — the ones at home — with the same intentionality applied to the classroom and the boardroom.

| Domain | Practice |
|---|---|
| Daily | Undivided presence: first and last hour of the day, no screens · One intentional act of fatherhood or partnership |
| Weekly | Family meeting or designated connect time · Reflection: am I more present this week than last? |

**Metrics**: Showed up when it counted (Y/N per day) · Building relationships or just managing logistics? (Weekly reflection)

**Decision filter**: *Does this business decision make me more or less available to the people who need me most?*

---

### FINANCE OS

**Purpose**: Steward resources to sustain the mission, generate generosity, and build legacy — not just personal wealth.

| Domain | Practice |
|---|---|
| Daily | DAME $ runs a revenue check — what came in, what's projected |
| Weekly | Revenue review: actual vs. projected · One generosity decision per week |
| Monthly | DAME $ full financial audit · Burn rate vs. revenue velocity |
| Quarterly | OKR review for revenue milestones · Reinvestment decision |

**Revenue ladder** (from OID):
$37 workbook → $97/yr community → $2,497 school PD → $4,997 standard → $10,997 summer institute → $20K+ district contract

**Decision filter**: *Does this investment serve the mission at scale, or does it serve my ego at expense?*

---

### FOCUS OS

**Purpose**: Protect the mind's capacity for deep work, learning, and long-term strategic thinking in a world designed to fragment both.

| Domain | Practice |
|---|---|
| Daily | 90-minute deep work block — calendar-protected, no notifications · One learning input per day (book chapter, idea, article) |
| Weekly | Reading review: what did I read, what did I retain, what changed? · Systems audit: is my AI infrastructure serving me or adding cognitive load? |

**Metrics**: Deep work hours per week (target: 10+ hrs) · Books/concepts absorbed per quarter · New system or framework built (Y/N per quarter)

**Decision filter**: *Does this new tool, idea, or commitment sharpen or dull my ability to think deeply?*

## PART 8 — THE DECISION ENGINE

Five filters. Every decision passes through all five, in order. The first failed filter ends the decision.

**Ask these questions before committing to any new project, product, partnership, opportunity, or tool.**

---

**FILTER 1 — THE MISSION FILTER**
*Does this serve the transformation mission?*

Mission: Help people transform their minds, bodies, purpose, and impact through faith-centered systems, education, fitness, leadership, and AI.

- PASS → Proceed to Filter 2.
- FAIL → Decline, defer, or delete.

---

**FILTER 2 — THE IDENTITY FILTER**
*Does this strengthen one of my 5 personal pillars, or does it deplete one?*

Pillars: Faith OS · Fitness OS · Family OS · Finance OS · Focus OS

- PASS → Strengthens at least one without depleting another. Proceed.
- FAIL → Depletes a pillar or requires compromising character for capability. Decline.

---

**FILTER 3 — THE DEPARTMENT FILTER**
*Which department does this belong to?*

- PASS → Clear department home. Route it there.
- CONDITIONAL → Strong mission alignment but no existing department. Flag to LINK for ecosystem evaluation.
- FAIL → Belongs nowhere. Mission alignment is weak. Decline.

---

**FILTER 4 — THE INFRASTRUCTURE FILTER**
*Can shared infrastructure handle this?*

- PASS → An AI agent, automation, template, or existing system handles the first pass. Route it.
- CONDITIONAL → Shared infrastructure handles 80%; Coach G handles the 20% only he can do.
- FAIL → Requires so much custom attention that it displaces higher-priority work with no clear ROI to the system. Defer or decline.

---

**FILTER 5 — THE EXECUTION FILTER**
*Is this human-only, AI-assisted, or automated?*

- **Human-only** → Deep Work Block. Treat with the same protection as Faith OS time.
- **AI-assisted** → Route through AI.G. Which specialist? Approve the output.
- **Automated** → Route to AUTOMATOR. Build the workflow. Never touch it again.
