# 2026–27 MONTHLY PROGRAM CALENDAR

**The detailed operating calendar.** Every month: the pillar, the theme, what happens each week, the experience, the artifact, the service, the B2M event, and what families need to do.

**How to read this:** every month runs the same four-week rhythm — **LEARN → DISCUSS → EXPERIENCE → CREATE.** Sessions are Sundays. Times and locations are confirmed monthly in the Parents & Guardians WhatsApp group and on the "Keeping Up With Da Village" calendar.

# NOVEMBER 2026 — SHOW ME THE MONEY
### LAMP Pillar: **LEADERSHIP** · 18-O Holes 10, 16

**The month's question:** *Where do Black professionals actually make money, and how did they get in?*

| Week | Beat | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | LEARN | The Money Trail. Trace revenue, expenses, customers, and value through one business end to end. Teach the nine Money Trail Questions and the Observation Lens until they're automatic. |
| **2** | DISCUSS | Golf-literate networking practice. Who do you want to meet and what are you going to ask them? Every Lamplighter prepares three questions and a 20-second self-introduction. Tier 2 runs the practice rounds. |
| **3** | EXPERIENCE | **Black Economy Career Night.** Professionals across golf, finance, real estate, hospitality, construction, tech, sales, sports management, and entrepreneurship. Lamplighters conduct the interviews — this is not a panel they watch. `[CONFIRM speakers + venue]` |
| **4** | CREATE | **Artifact: Money Trail Map.** Each Lamplighter maps one industry: who owns, who operates, who buys, who gets paid, and where he could enter. |

**Signature Experience:** Black Economy Career Night
**Artifact:** Money Trail Map
**Service:** Thanksgiving-season service `[CONFIRM — House of Mercy is the Year One relationship]`
**B2M Event:** *"I Follow the Money."* Age-appropriate money trail — Lamplighters walk B2M through who gets paid inside one business they already know.
**Family Action:** Invite one professional you know to Career Night. One introduction from each family fills the room.
