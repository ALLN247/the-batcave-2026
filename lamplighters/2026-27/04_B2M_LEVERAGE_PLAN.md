# THE B2M LEVERAGE PLAN
## How Boys 2 Men Powers Lamplighters Year Two

**Rochester Omega Lamplighters × Boys 2 Men × UPrep Golf · 2026–27**
Prepared by Coach G

---

## THE STRATEGIC READ

Most people look at the B2M partnership and see community service. That's the small version.

**B2M solves three of the chapter's four Year Two problems at once.**

| Chapter Problem | How B2M Solves It |
|---|---|
| **Not enough committed adult volunteers** | B2M families are already bought in, already know Coach G, and already have sons in the pipeline. They are the highest-probability volunteer pool in Rochester — and nobody has formally asked them. |
| **Tier 2 Lamplighters need real leadership reps, not worksheets** | Planning and running a monthly event for 9-to-13-year-olds is the single best leadership laboratory available, and it costs the chapter almost nothing. |
| **Recruitment pipeline for the next class** | 39 boys ages 9–13 are already in the program. The oldest are 13 — they age into Lamplighters eligibility *next year*. |
| *(Not solved by B2M: meeting space and budget — those need separate work.)* | |

Coach G directs both programs. That is not a scheduling coincidence — **it is the leverage.**

---

## THE PIPELINE, MADE VISIBLE

**Boys 2 Men (9–13) → Rochester Omega Lamplighters (14–18) → Omega Psi Phi (adult)**

A 10-year-old at 585 Dream Camp who watches a 16-year-old Lamplighter run the room doesn't have to *imagine* the pipeline. He is looking at it. And when UPrep Golf student-athletes are in that room too, he sees it from two directions instead of one.

**This is a three-way mentoring structure, not a hand-off.**

## HOW IT WORKS

### THE CADENCE

| Window | Cadence | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **School year (Sept–June)** | One signature 18 Opportunities event per month, mirroring that month's Lamplighters 18-O theme | 585 Dream Camp, 321 Post Ave |
| **Summer (July–August)** | Biweekly, layered into B2M's existing M–F day-camp block | 585 Dream Camp |

**Start monthly. Treat biweekly as the target once the facilitator bench is built and the first few events run clean.** A cadence that actually happens every time beats a more frequent one that starts slipping.

### THE CONTENT RULE

Content adapts the 18-O monthly theme — belonging, excellence, relationships, purpose, leadership, legacy — into age-appropriate B2M activities. **Most events run through B2M's existing 9-section Program Framework with an opportunity theme layered on top.** Golf is one occasional activity lane, not the spine.

**This adds to what B2M already runs. It does not replace it.**

### THE EVENT SHAPE (90 minutes, matching B2M's block structure)

| Time | Segment | Who Runs It |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Circle Up — welcome, standard, theme of the month | Tier 2 Lamplighter |
| 0:10–0:30 | Teach — the scaled-down 18-O concept | Tier 2 Lamplighter + UPrep Golf athlete |
| 0:30–1:00 | Stations — 2–3 rotations (skill, money trail, build/pitch) | Tier 1 Lamplighters + golf athletes |
| 1:00–1:20 | Build / Do — B2M boys produce something | Facilitator bench |
| 1:20–1:30 | Money Trail Debrief + Close | Tier 2 Lamplighter |

**Every event ends with the same four questions:** What did you see? Who gets paid? What skills did those people need? Where could you enter?

## THE VOLUNTEER PLAY (THE PART EVERYONE SKIPS)

**Every B2M event is a volunteer recruitment event.** Run it deliberately:

1. **Invite B2M parents to attend** the monthly event, not just drop off.
2. **Give them something to watch** — their son being led by a young man who was in his seat four years ago.
3. **Make one clear ask at the close.** Not "we need help." Specifically: *"We need two adults who can give us four hours a month. Here's exactly what that looks like. Who's in?"*
4. **Capture the commitment in writing that day** — name, role, hours, months.
5. **Follow up within 48 hours** with the onboarding packet.

**Target: 4–6 new committed adult volunteers from the B2M family pool by December 2026.** That is what unlocks the class size the chapter actually wants.

## WHAT EACH SIDE GAINS

**B2M gains** structured, opportunity-framed programming delivered by near-peer role models just a few years older — the pipeline becomes something a 10-year-old can see, and he sees it from two directions instead of one.

**Lamplighters gains** real leadership and facilitation reps for its own members, documented service hours with substance, a pre-qualified recruitment pool, and access to the most likely source of new adult volunteers in the city.

**UPrep Golf gains** a direct mentoring role for its student-athletes and proof the 18-O framework works as a youth-development model beyond one sport.
